# Adam's Dashboard

Adam's Dashboard is a personal project that reads my own Google Health (Fitbit) data, such as daily step counts, and shows it on a private dashboard on my home network.

## Privacy Policy

- The app only accesses the Google account of its developer (me), and only read-only Google Health data.
- The data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone else. It is not used for advertising.
- Data is stored only on my own devices.
- The app's use of information received from Google Health APIs complies with the Google Health API data policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
